## Otpusk project status and getting started

This handover is an example template in the Otpusk working draft. To explore it,
run `pnpm install` and `pnpm dev` from the repository root, open
http://127.0.0.1:5173, sign in with a fictional account from the project decision
journal, and open Corporate documents. A built local preview uses `pnpm build`
and `pnpm start`.

The main leave-tracking criteria are covered. The next planned work includes
email reminders, corporate events and parties, private employee documents such
as medical certificates and employment contracts, financial transparency for
salary and promotion possibilities, and integrations with Teams, Jira, banking,
and other company tools. These areas remain future work because cloud accounts,
financial information, production databases, and mandatory corporate workflows
require additional decisions and complex integration work.
