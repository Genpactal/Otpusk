# Welcome to Forma Studio

Mock corporate document · Version 1.0 · Updated 13 September 2026
Owner: People team · Audience: All employees

## Your first week

- Meet your assigned manager and agree on your first-week priorities.
- Check your team, start date, and manager in Otpusk under My profile.
- Ask your manager for access to the tools your role needs.
- Read the leave guide and the information security checklist.
- Arrange a short introduction with your teammates.

## Your workspace

Overview shows your leave entitlement and upcoming time off. My requests keeps
your requests and their history together. Team calendar shows approved leave.
Corporate documents holds these shared guides and templates.

## Getting help

Contact the People team for profile corrections or onboarding questions.
Ask your assigned manager about priorities and leave approvals.
The contact directory contains fictional examples for this local draft.

This is sample content for the Otpusk demo, not an adopted company policy.

## Otpusk project status

Otpusk is currently a working draft. The main leave-tracking criteria are covered:
employee profiles, accrual, requests, manager review, calendars, HR overview,
notifications, reminders, cancellation, rescheduling, login, shared sessions,
and this corporate document library.

### Getting started with the draft

From the repository root, run `pnpm install`, then `pnpm dev`. Open
http://127.0.0.1:5173 and sign in with one of the fictional accounts recorded in
the project decision journal. Employees can use their own workspace; HR and
managers can switch demo account views. Open Corporate documents to browse this
library. The built preview uses `pnpm build` followed by `pnpm start`.

### What comes next

Planned extensions include email reminders; corporate events and parties;
employee-specific records such as medical certificates and employment contracts;
financial transparency such as current salary and possible promotions; and
integrations with the company's tools, such as Microsoft Teams, Jira, and banking
systems.

These items are not in the draft because external accounts, financial data,
production databases, and mandatory corporate workflows need additional product
decisions, security work, and integration complexity. The current draft focuses
on the main criteria and is ready for local review and straightforward deployment
once those production decisions are made.
