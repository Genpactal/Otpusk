# Leave guide

Mock corporate document · Version 1.0 · Updated 13 September 2026
Owner: People team · Audience: All employees

## Earning leave

The Otpusk draft accrues 28 calendar days per full year of employment, daily
using each year's actual length. Paid leave and weekends continue to accrue
entitlement. Unused entitlement carries forward without expiry.

## Planning a request

Use the selected-date forecast to see your projected balance. Your complete
request must be affordable by its first leave day, after other commitments.
Each segment must contain at least one whole calendar day. Weekends and public
holidays count. There is no mandatory 14-day segment in this draft.

## Approval

Your assigned manager approves or rejects the complete request with a comment.
Pending requests reserve days. Only approved dates appear on the calendar.
The manager sees overlapping approved leave from your team before deciding.

## Changing plans

Withdraw a pending request or cancel upcoming approved leave to release days.
After leave starts, cancellation keeps dates through today charged and returns
only future unused dates. Completed leave cannot be changed.
A reschedule or return-to-work change needs manager approval; the return date
is the first day back. Existing approval remains until a replacement is approved.

## Notifications

The in-app inbox records request and decision notifications. The default reminder
is three calendar days before the first segment, once for you and your manager.

This document summarizes the implemented mock leave rules. It does not establish
an employment contract or a real company's policy.

## Otpusk project status and getting started

This leave guide describes the implemented rules in an Otpusk working draft. To
try it, run `pnpm install` and `pnpm dev` from the repository root, open
http://127.0.0.1:5173, and sign in with a fictional account from the project
decision journal. The draft's main criteria are covered and the local built
preview can be run with `pnpm build` and `pnpm start`.

Future work includes email reminders, corporate events and parties, private
employee records such as medical certificates and employment contracts, financial
transparency, and integrations with Teams, Jira, banking systems, and other
company tools. These are intentionally not implemented yet because external
accounts, financial data, cloud connections, and mandatory corporate workflows
need further security, product, and integration decisions.
