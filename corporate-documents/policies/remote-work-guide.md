# Remote work guide

Mock corporate document · Version 1.0 · Updated 13 September 2026
Owner: People team · Audience: All employees

## Planning your day

Agree on your working location and availability with your manager. Add focus
time to your work calendar and keep your team informed when plans change.
Choose a workspace where you can take calls without exposing company information.

## Staying connected

- Share a short update on your current priorities in your team's usual channel.
- Tell teammates when you will be unavailable for a scheduled meeting.
- Agree on a handover when a task needs coverage during time off.
- Use Otpusk for leave requests; working remotely is not a leave request.

## Equipment

Use your approved work device and follow the information security checklist.
Contact the example help desk if a device or connection prevents you from working.

These are illustrative guidelines for the demo only. Remote-work scheduling and
attendance tracking are not implemented in Otpusk.

## Otpusk project status and getting started

This guide is mock content inside the Otpusk working draft. Start the project from
its repository root with `pnpm install`, then `pnpm dev`; open
http://127.0.0.1:5173 and sign in with a fictional account listed in the project
decision journal. The draft can also be built and served locally with
`pnpm build` and `pnpm start`.

The next planned areas are email reminders, corporate events and parties,
employee-specific medical and employment documents, salary and promotion
transparency, and integrations with Teams, Jira, banking, and other company
systems. They are not part of this draft because connecting cloud accounts,
financial information, production databases, and mandatory corporate functions
requires deeper security and integration design.
